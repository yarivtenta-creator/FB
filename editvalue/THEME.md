# Aisle

A custom Theme Factory theme. Soft, unhurried and photography-led — a linen
invitation rather than a grading suite. Built for EditValue, whose product is
wedding films, and whose case is craft and taste rather than throughput.

## Colour palette

- **Bone**: `#F6F3EE` — warm off-white, the page ground
- **Deep Olive**: `#4A5340` — primary; the one saturated field on the page
- **Antique Brass**: `#B08D57` — accent; rules, numerals, small marks
- **Umber**: `#2E2A26` — text and anchors

Supporting neutrals are all pulled toward the bone rather than toward pure
grey, so nothing on the page reads as un-chosen. The dark theme rotates the
same four hues rather than inverting them: the ground becomes an olive-biased
near-black, olive lifts to a sage that survives on it, and brass warms.

## Typography

- **Display**: Cormorant Garamond, weight 300, set very large with tight
  tracking. It is a delicate face — below about 28px it stops earning its keep,
  so it is never used for anything small.
- **Body**: Jost, weight 300–400, with a touch of positive letter-spacing.
  A geometric sans under a high-contrast serif keeps the page from tipping into
  costume.
- **Accent**: Cormorant Garamond italic for pull quotes and the one or two
  words per page that carry emphasis.
- **Labels**: Jost 500, uppercase, `.22em` tracking — small caps in feel,
  used with a hairline rule the way an order of service sets its sections.

All three faces are self-hosted variable woff2 (latin subset) in
`assets/fonts/`. No third-party request at runtime.

## Rules of the direction

1. **Air is the material.** Section padding is large and never compressed to
   fit more in. If something has to be squeezed, it gets cut instead.
2. **Hairlines, not boxes.** Structure is drawn with 1px rules and whitespace.
   There are no cards, no shadows, no filled panels except one.
3. **One saturated field.** The risk-free offer is the only full-bleed olive
   section. It is the page's single raised voice, so nothing else competes.
4. **Brass is punctuation.** Used on rules, numerals and marks — never on
   large areas, never as a button fill on light ground.
5. **Centre deliberately.** The hero and the offer are centred because an
   invitation is. Everything else sits on a strong left margin.

## Best used for

Wedding and event brands, photography and film portfolios, boutique studios,
hospitality — anywhere the product is craft and the buyer needs to feel taste
before they read a specification.
