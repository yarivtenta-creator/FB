The problem

Most couples wait three to six months to see their own wedding.

Not because the edit takes that long. Because it sits in a queue behind next Saturday, and the one after that. Season fills up, the backlog grows, and the film that mattered most to somebody lands in their inbox when the flowers are long gone.

Meanwhile you’re editing at midnight instead of booking. The couple has stopped posting about the day. The tag that would have brought you the next three enquiries never happens.
