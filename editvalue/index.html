The wait

Most couples wait three to six months to see their own wedding.

Not because the edit takes that long. Because it sits behind next Saturday, and the one after. The season fills, the backlog grows, and the film that mattered most to somebody arrives when the flowers are long gone.

Meanwhile you are editing at midnight instead of booking. The couple has stopped posting about the day. The tag that would have brought you the next three enquiries never happens.

The ten days

Ten days, and we have never missed one.

A thousand weddings a year is enough volume that the schedule is a machine rather than a promise. Every wedding is placed the day it arrives.

How it works

Four steps, and only one of them is yours.

  1. One

    Send the footage

    Upload the cards however you already do. Tell us the song, and anything the couple asked for by name.

  2. Two

    We cut it

    You hear from us when there is something to watch, and not before. No status to chase.

  3. Three

    You give notes

    Two full rounds of revisions are included. Pacing, music, moments in or out — say it plainly and it is done.

  4. Four

    Deliver it as yours

    Files arrive with you unbranded. It goes to the couple under your studio's name. We stay invisible.
