The EditValue difference

Editing Is Where the Story Becomes Real.

Anyone can assemble footage in the order it was shot. The work that matters happens after that — finding the moment the day actually turned, the rhythm the music was asking for, the look on a face that makes the whole film land. That material is already in your rushes. Our job is to find it and get out of its way.

  1. 01

    Story

    Every wedding, film and campaign has one thread that holds it together. We find that first and cut everything else to serve it.

  2. 02

    Rhythm

    Pace is the difference between a film people watch and a film people finish. Every cut is checked against the track, and against the breath before it.

  3. 03

    Emotion

    The grade, the mix and the silence around a line do more than any transition. We build toward the moment somebody watching forgets they are watching.

How it works

Five steps. One of them
is yours.

  1. 01

    Send Your Footage

    Upload the cards however you already do. Mixed cameras and frame rates are fine — 4K in, Full HD out.

  2. 02

    We Find the Story

    Everything is logged and synced, then we watch it properly before a single cut is made.

  3. 03

    Editing and Refinement

    Cut, sound and grade. You hear from us when there is something worth watching, not before.

  4. 04

    Review and Feedback

    Two full rounds of revisions, included. Pacing, music, moments in or out.

  5. 05

    Final Delivery

    Unbranded masters plus a social cut-down, delivered in ten days. Renders held on our servers for three months.
